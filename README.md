## 🧠 My Security Investigation Mindset

When investigating an alert, I try to answer:

```text
What happened?
      ↓
Which process / account / host was involved?
      ↓
What caused it?
      ↓
What happened before and after?
      ↓
Is the behavior expected?
      ↓
What evidence supports the conclusion?
      ↓
What should happen next?
```

I’m particularly interested in moving beyond **“what does this log mean?”** toward understanding **how individual events connect into an attack story.**
